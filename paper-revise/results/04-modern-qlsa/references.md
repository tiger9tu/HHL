[Versioned primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.22185v2), [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.22185v2), DOI [10.48550/arXiv.2604.22185](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2604.22185). Cite as a preprint. Its appearance after the referee report dated 10 March 2026 is consistent with adding it during revision.

Reference 2 tests small random systems, including 32×32 and 64×64 matrices. Its unknown-norm comparison uses exhaustive log-space search (Algorithm 3 of reference 1), rather than the optimal Algorithm 4. Query/polynomial order is its principal cost measure; the inspected paper supplies no application-specific compiled Clifford+T count, synthesis precision budget, routing schedule or QEC-ready depth. The plan's claim of a T-gate analysis should therefore be corrected. Its observed quantum-walk advantage with unknown norm is evidence about those tests, not a worst-case guarantee or PDE extrapolation. See Sec. III, especially the distinction between known- and unknown-norm experiments and the query-cost convention for non-Hermitian inputs.

Selection: use reference 1 Algorithm 4 for an unknown-norm, asymptotically optimal comparison with explicit finite constants. Use Algorithm 1 only as a separately certified norm-informed scenario. Reference 2 motivates sensitivity to algorithm choice; it does not establish that our selected solver is practically fastest. Do not substitute its empirical prefactors into the rigorous bound or claim its experiments benchmark Algorithm 4.

The manuscript PDF's introduction excludes loading/readout; p. 3 Eqs. (1)–(2) and its resource discussion use the old HHL prefactor. `tools/surface_code_opt.py:HHL` implements that prefactor and a fixed ancilla assumption. `src/HHL/HamiltonianEvolution/Oracle/Oracle.qs` generates lookup operations by traversing dense host arrays and nonzero entries. It is neither a unit-cost sparse oracle nor a constructed QSVT block encoding. Task 03 already isolates a replacement HHL core. We read its resource model, input and handoff and preserve its distinction between a controlled Hamiltonian-simulation call and an A-access query. No direct ratio of these different counters is meaningful.

`input-versions.json` hashes the actual PDFs, task prompts, interfaces and relevant code. PDF text extraction sometimes splits words or mangles mathematical symbols; equations for this model were checked against the primary arXiv HTML as well.
